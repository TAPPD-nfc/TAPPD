    How it works.

    A coaster, two stickers, and one tap.

    QR code stickerNFC stickerAcrylic coaster
    1
    Place the coaster.

    An acrylic coaster with an NFC sticker, topped by a QR code sticker, sits on every table.

    2
    Guest taps their phone.

    One tap on the coaster. If their phone can’t tap, they scan the QR code instead.

    3
    Your page opens.

    A website made for your restaurant opens with your logo, menu, review link, payment options and Wi-Fi.

    Questions.

    Do guests need an app?

    No. They tap their phone on the coaster and the page opens in the browser.

    What if a phone can’t tap?

    Each coaster has a QR code sticker on top. Scanning it opens the same page.

    Is Tappd only for restaurants?

    No. It works for cafés, bars, hotels, salons, shops, events and any business where guests sit down.

    Can the page use our logo?

    Yes. Every page is customized for the business and shows your logo and your links.
